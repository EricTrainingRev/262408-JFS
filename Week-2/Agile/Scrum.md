## User Stories and Story Points
User Stories are a way of describing features/functionality from the user perspective
- As a...
    - who or what is performing the action
- I want ...
    - what action is being performed
- So that...
    - what is the end goal of the story
As a customer I want to login so that I can score points on my coffee purchase
- given the customer is on the login page
- when the customer submits their credentials
- then the customer should see their information on the purchase page

Story Points are assigned to user stories to help gauge how much effort it will take to complete the user story
- fibbonaci sequence
- hours you think it will take
- number of classes involved
- etc.

Always keep in mind these points are estimates: they are meant to help you and your team gauge how much effort your work will take, and therefore, how many user stories your team can tackle during a sprint

Most Agile methodologies will have some kind of task board to manage and track work. There are multiple categories for user stories and their associated work to go in
- Project Backlog
- Sprint board
- Todo board
- In progress board
- Review board
- Completed board

A Burn-Down chart is a way of tracking whether or not you are completing work at a pace to accomplish all work by the end of the Sprint
- x axis of the graph is # of days in the sprint
- y axis is story points for the sprint
