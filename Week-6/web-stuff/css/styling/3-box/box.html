The box: size, and inner/outer space

width and height give me fixed dimensions (260 x 80).

padding is space inside me, between the text and the edge.

margin gives me space all around, outside the border.

border outlines me, and border-radius rounds my corners.

padding: the pale blue area includes my padding, so it wraps the text.

margin: the pale blue area is just my content; my margin is the clear space around it.
