# Get Started

Before writing any code, gather the user's requirements by asking a series of
pointed questions. Do not build until you have answers. Ask questions one or a
few at a time, and let the user's answers guide which follow-ups you ask.

## What to ask about

### 1. Purpose & audience
- What is the site for? (personal brand, portfolio, resume, blog, contact page…)
- Who is the intended audience?

### 2. Content & sections
- What sections should the page have? (e.g. About, Projects, Experience, Contact)
- What specific content will you provide (name, bio, links, projects)?
- Do you have existing copy, or should the agent draft it?

### 3. Layout & structure
- Single page with sections, or multiple pages?
- Any preference for navigation style (sticky header, top nav, sidebar)?

### 4. Design preferences
- Beyond the silver/gold/red palette, any other style preferences?
  (minimal, bold, elegant, playful…)
- Typography or spacing preferences?
- Any reference sites you like the look of?

### 5. Contact & links
- What contact info and social/portfolio links should appear?

### 6. Scope & constraints
- Anything to explicitly include or avoid?
- Any accessibility or responsiveness requirements?

## Rules
- Ask pointed, specific questions — not vague ones.
- Do not start building until the user has answered the questions that matter
  for the task.
- Record the answers and follow them when building.
